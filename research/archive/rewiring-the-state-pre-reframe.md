## Why it is no longer served as the current portfolio version

The portfolio's editorial framing was subsequently changed. The current public version treats *Rewiring the State* as policy context and asks what evidence says about expected-benefit pathways, implementation conditions, uncertainty and monitoring. It does not present a verdict on the political desirability of current government policy.

Nothing in the original research has been purged from Git history. If a later review or authorisation process requires the exact pre-reframe package, restore it from the commit above rather than reconstructing it from memory.
